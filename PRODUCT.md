## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers screening Navin Byron Llanes for internships and junior front-end / UI roles. They skim quickly, usually on a laptop between other candidates, sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn or résumé link. Their job: judge skills and proof of work in under a minute, then find a way to make contact.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Navin Byron Llanes, Computer Science student at Lyceum of the Philippines University – Manila and working student. Success = a recruiter understands who Navin is, what he builds, and how to reach him, and leaves with a memorable impression.

## Positioning
A front-end/UI-minded CS student who also works retail and leads in student esports orgs. The one thing no template portfolio has: an in-page typing test where visitors race Navin's own run (92 wpm / 97% accuracy).

## Operating Context
Static site, deployed to Vercel or Netlify (domain not yet chosen). Linked from GitHub, LinkedIn and résumés.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections: home/intro, projects, experience, stack, certifications & seminars.
- Contact: email modal with copy-to-clipboard and mailto; GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram links; phone and location shown.
- Typing test ("type-check"): 30 s timed test, live wpm/accuracy, on-screen keyboard, result card comparing against Navin's 92 wpm / 97%.
- Dark and light themes, persisted per visitor.
- Links without a real destination (certificate verification, some project repos) are hidden until real URLs exist; never link to "#".

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Navin (display), full name Navin Byron Llanes.
- Personal photo: `barko.jpg`.
- Typing-test challenge stays a signature feature.

## Evidence on Hand
- Projects: LIBR-AI-RY, IoT Intrusion Detection (Random Forest 98.42% accuracy on IoT-23), CoffeeQuest / KoFind, Red Warriors Ordering & POS.
- Experience: H&M Sales Advisor; Lyceum Pirate Esports Organization committees; work immersion leader.
- Certificate images in `src/assets/certs/`.
- No testimonials, metrics beyond the above, or employer endorsements exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over adjectives: show real projects and certificates, not claims.
2. Contact is never more than one action away.
3. Personality through interaction (the typing race), not decoration.
4. Fast and readable on a phone opened from a LinkedIn link.
